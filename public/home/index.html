Your local community is full of talent. Most of it is still in school.

We started Grassroots to give that talent somewhere to go. Local businesses get a helping hand with everything from social media to software development, and students get experience that’s hard to find in a classroom, working for the community they’re becoming part of.

How it works

  1. Post a project

    A local business writes a short brief: what they need, a budget range, and a timeline. It takes about five minutes, and posting is free.

  2. Students bid

    College students nearby read the brief and send a short pitch with their price and plan. The business compares bids at its own pace.

  3. Pick a bid

    The business picks the student who fits best and funds the project. Grassroots holds the payment, processed by Stripe, until the work is done.

  4. Approve and get paid

    The student delivers the work and makes revisions if needed. Once the business approves it, the student gets paid and both sides leave a review.

Things people ask before posting

How does pricing work?

Students send offers with their own price, and you pick one. You pay the offer plus a 10% platform fee when you accept, and we hold it until you approve the work. Until March 1, 2027, the fee is 10% 5% as a launch rate.

What kinds of students are on Grassroots?

College students at four-year and two-year schools, mostly juniors and seniors. School email verification is coming. Each profile lists school, major, and past work. You see a student's profile when they send an offer on your project.

What happens if I don't approve the work?

Report a problem from the project page and we lock the payment. We review the work against the agreed scope. Then we release it, have the student redo it, or refund you. See the refund policy.

Can I hire the same student again?

Yes. You can post again directly to a past student, set up a retainer contract, or open a new public project and invite them to send an offer.

Are taxes and 1099s handled?

Payouts go to students through Stripe. Grassroots issues tax forms to students where the law requires it. You'll get a clean year end summary of what you paid through the platform for your own books.

What cities are you in?

Grassroots is opening town by town. New towns open as students and businesses there join.

We launched in 14 college towns across Florida, Georgia, and the Carolinas. New towns open as students and businesses there join.
