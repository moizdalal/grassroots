small businesses have real work to do, but agencies cost too much and take too long.

grassroots pairs them with talented college students nearby. from logos and menus to websites and social posts, the work gets done well and students build experience that pays.

how it works.

  1. post a project

    a local business writes a short brief: what they need, a budget range, and a timeline. it takes about five minutes, and posting is free.

  2. students bid

    vetted college students nearby read the brief and send a short pitch with their price and plan. the business compares bids at its own pace.

  3. pick a bid

    the business picks the student who fits best and funds the project. the money is held safely through Stripe until the work is done.

  4. approve and get paid

    the student delivers the work and makes revisions if needed. once the business approves it, the student gets paid and both sides leave a review.

protected payments for both sides.

the money is held securely by Stripe, so both sides are covered.

things people ask before posting.

how does pricing work?

students bid their own price on each project; you pick the bid you like. there's no platform fee until a job is funded. once it is, grassroots takes a flat 10% of the contract value, published up front on every bid, so students keep 90% of what they earn.

what kinds of students are on grassroots?

verified college students at four year and two year schools, mostly juniors and seniors. each profile lists school, major, and past work. you can filter by skill, school, or city before you ever post.

can i hire the same student again?

yes. you can post again directly to a past consultant, set up a retainer contract, or open a new public listing and invite them to bid. most repeat posters lock in a retainer after two projects.

are taxes and 1099s handled?

payouts go to students through Stripe, which tracks earnings and issues 1099s where required. you'll get a clean end of year summary of what you paid through the platform for your own books.

what cities are you in?

grassroots is opening town by town. new towns open when there's a critical mass of students and businesses; you can request your city from the footer.
