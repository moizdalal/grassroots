how it works

four steps. nothing weird.

scope the work, pick a bid, fund escrow, approve the result. no recruiter calls. no agency intake forms.

/ 04
step one

post a project

write a short brief. set a budget range. pick one-off, phased, or retainer. takes about five minutes.

step two

get scoped bids

local students send a short pitch and a price. read them at your own pace. no recruiter calls.

step three

fund escrow

pick a bid. fund the agreed amount through Stripe. it sits there, untouched, until you say so.

step four

release on approval

review the work. ask for changes if needed. release funds when it's yours. the student gets paid same day.

questions

things people ask before posting.

how does pricing work?

students bid their own price on each project; you pick the bid you like. there's no platform fee until a job is funded. once it is, grassroots takes a small percentage of the contract value — published up front on every bid.

what kinds of students are on grassroots?

verified college students at four-year and two-year schools, mostly juniors and seniors. each profile lists school, major, and past work. you can filter by skill, school, or city before you ever post.

what happens if i don't approve the work?

funds stay in escrow. you request changes through the engagement page. if you and the student can't agree, our support team mediates and can refund the escrow if the work hasn't met the agreed scope.

can i hire the same student again?

yes. you can re-post directly to a past consultant, set up a retainer contract, or open a new public listing and invite them to bid. most repeat posters lock in a retainer after two projects.

are taxes and 1099s handled?

payouts go to students through Stripe, which tracks earnings and issues 1099s where required. you'll get a clean year-end summary of what you paid through the platform for your own books.

what cities are you in?

we launched in 14 college towns across Florida, Georgia, and the Carolinas. new towns open when there's a critical mass of students and businesses; you can request your city from the footer.
