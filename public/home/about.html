our mission

small business growth powered by students

grassroots connects small businesses with high-intent student talent so meaningful work, practical experience, and local growth happen at the same time.

MD

meet the founder

moiz dalal

While serving on the leadership team of the UF Rho Epsilon Mu (REM) on-campus chapter, Moiz saw firsthand how many small businesses were desperate for consultative services like marketing strategy, financial planning, and operations support but could not afford traditional agency rates.

At the same time, he noticed an incredible pool of motivated students eager to apply what they were learning in real-world settings and hungry for experience, portfolio pieces, and the kind of work that actually moves the needle.

grassroots was born from that realization: the gap between small business need and student ambition is a bridge worth building. By connecting businesses with talented consultants at accessible price points, grassroots creates a win-win with real growth for businesses and invaluable experience for consultants.
