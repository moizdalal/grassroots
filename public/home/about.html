about us

the local marketplace connecting small businesses with college students nearby.

who we are

grassroots is a local marketplace that connects small businesses with college students nearby. it's built for the towns where local students and local businesses live and work side by side.

what we do

businesses post the projects they need help with, like logos, menus, websites, and social posts. students nearby bid with a price and a plan, the business picks the best fit, and payment is held securely by Stripe until the work is done.

our mission

small businesses keep a town running, but good help is hard to afford. college students want real work, but internships and jobs are so competitive that many never get the chance to build experience. our mission is to connect the two, so businesses get affordable help and students get experience that sets them apart.
