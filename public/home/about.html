Who we are

A local marketplace for small businesses and college students.

What we do

Businesses post projects, and students nearby bid with a price and a plan. Grassroots holds the payment until the work is done.

Our mission

Grow small businesses while giving students real-world experience, and help the whole community develop along the way.

Meet Moiz.

Founder of Grassroots

I'm a double Gator. I studied finance as an undergrad at UF and stayed for a master's in information systems. Most of my time there went to pro bono consulting, and working with small businesses was always the part I loved most.

I started Grassroots to close a gap I kept seeing. Students wanted real experience, small businesses needed real help, and towns missed out when the two never met. I think the answer is ownership: students run their own projects, and businesses get better work, faster, from people who can move quickly.

The mission is to give students more ways to get involved in their community and help it grow, because at the end of the day this is for small businesses. Students leave with work they can talk about on a resume or in an interview. Businesses support local students and get real work back in return.
