Why join

  • Get paid for real work

    You set the price on every project, and real businesses pay you for the work you do for them.

  • Build a real resume

    You run the project yourself, work directly with the owner, and finish with real experience you can put on your resume.

  • Earn real references

    A local owner who has seen your work can vouch for you for years.

How it works

It only takes a few steps to go from applying to your first project.

  1. Get in

    You apply with your .edu email, a past project you're proud of and a few lines on why you want to help small businesses.

  2. Build your bio

    You add your school, your skills, and what you want to work on so owners can get to know you.

  3. Bid your price

    You pick a local listing, send a short pitch, and name your price.

  4. Ship the work

    You do the work for the business and stay in touch with the owner until it is finished.

  5. Get paid and reviewed

    You get paid once the work is approved, and the business reviews you on six dimensions.

How you get paid

You set your price and get paid once the business approves the work. Grassroots keeps a flat 10% of your offer, and transaction processing comes out of that, not your payout. The rest is yours.

Things students ask before applying

What kind of work is actually on here?

You'll find short, scoped projects from local businesses, like logo refreshes, menu redesigns, Shopify cleanups, market research, social retainers, copy passes, and product photos. You can see them all in the marketplace once you're in, and none of it is weird or exploitative.

How much can I realistically earn?

You set your own price on every bid, and you keep 90% of it. How many projects you take on each semester is up to you. Retainer clients can run all year, so the ceiling is yours.

Who owns the work after?

The business does, once the payment is released. You can still list it on your resume and show it on your profile, unless the business opts out in writing first.

Does Grassroots take a cut?

Yes, a flat 10% platform fee, taken from your payout. Transaction processing is included in that 10%, not taken on top. There's no subscription, per-bid fee or payout fee. The rest is yours.

Can I quit anytime?

Yes, you can take a semester off or finish a project and disappear for a year, and your profile and reviews will stay where they are.
