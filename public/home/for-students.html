Things students ask before applying

What kind of work is actually on here?

Short, scoped projects from local businesses: logo refreshes, menu redesigns, Shopify cleanups, market research, social retainers, copy passes, product photos. You'll see them all in the marketplace once you're in. Nothing weird, nothing exploitative.

Do I need an "impressive" portfolio to get in?

No. We want students with taste, hustle, and care — not students with a 200-page Behance. One thing you made and a paragraph about why you'd be great for Grassroots is enough. People on the referral path get in with zero portfolio.

How much can I realistically earn?

You set your own price on every bid, and the platform fee is 10% of funded work above the first $10 (5% during launch). How many projects you take on each semester is up to you. Retainer clients can run all year. The ceiling is yours.

Who owns the work after?

You do. The business gets a license to use it; you keep full IP and full portfolio rights. Show it everywhere. It's in the contract.

What if I don't have a city listed?

Apply anyway. We open new towns when there's a critical mass of students + businesses. You and three other applications can literally open your town.

Does Grassroots take a cut?

10% platform fee on the part above $10, 5% during launch, only on funded work. Card processing comes out too. No monthly subscription, no per-bid fee, no payout fee. The rest is yours.

Can I quit anytime?

Yes. Take a semester off, finish a project and disappear for a year, whatever. Your profile and reviews stay where they are.
