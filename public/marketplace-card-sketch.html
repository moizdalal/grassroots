notes

  • Recency placement: right-aligned eyebrow above the title. Dot prefix (green = fresh, neutral = stale) gives visual freshness without numbers. Stays clear of the title's wrap zone.
  • Title scale: clamp(1.6rem, 3.2vw, 2.1rem). Bigger than v1 but capped at 2 lines so long titles don't double the card height.
  • Avatar tile: 96×96 to balance against the bigger title. Real avatar fills; fallback shows lowercase initials in moss on accent-soft.
  • Card height: ~280px (was ~240 in v1, ~140 before any redesign). On a 3-column grid that's ~3 cards above the fold instead of 4. Worth confirming the trade is right before shipping.
  • What's missing here that v1 has: the hover-summary popover. Easy to add — same fixed-positioned shared element, just point its anchor to .v2card.
