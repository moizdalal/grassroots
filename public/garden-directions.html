Garden background directions

Four simple backdrops for the garden on your profile, each shown with the same sample projects. Flowers at earlier stages are included so you can see how a growing plant would look. Paper is now the live backdrop, with bees in the light theme and fireflies in the dark themes.

Theme

1Paper

A plain card and one soft ground line. The quietest option: nothing competes with the flowers.

2Field notebook

A dot-grid plot, like a page from a grower's notebook. Ties the garden to the "field office" feel of the rest of the app.

3Horizon

A soft wash of green that gathers at the ground. Keeps a hint of outdoors without any scenery.

4Specimen shelf

A framed card with the flowers standing on a shelf and name tags below. Reads as a collection you have earned.

Today's background, for comparison

The current sky and hills (stars, moon and fireflies in dark themes; sun, clouds and bees in light).
