Pick the right contract

Three options. Pick the one that matches the shape of the work.

Default

Standard

One task, one payment, one delivery.

Good for Short, scoped jobs you can describe in a sentence.

Examples A logo, a one-time market push, a single research memo.

How it works The consultant submits one offer. You accept, they deliver, you pay once.

Advanced

Phase-based

Proposal first, then the solution. Add maintenance if you need it.

Good for Projects where you want to see the plan before paying for the build.

Examples A brand refresh, a market-entry plan, a content calendar with execution.

How it works Two locked phases (proposal → solution) plus an optional maintenance phase. Each phase is its own deliverable and its own payment.

Advanced

Retainer

Ongoing work on a repeating cadence.

Good for Recurring workflows where rhythm matters more than a fixed deliverable.

Examples Weekly social, monthly bookkeeping, ongoing SEO, fractional support.

How it works You describe cadence and scope in the listing. The consultant bills on the agreed interval until either side ends it.

Still unsure?

Start with standard. You can post a second listing with an advanced contract once you know what shape the work needs to take.
