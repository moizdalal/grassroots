# reviews & ratings

> after an engagement wraps, the business owner rates the consultant on five dimensions. this is the signal grassroots uses to surface trusted consultants in search and to drive the stage-progression ladder.

## who rates whom

only the **business owner** rates the consultant. consultants don't rate businesses today.

a review is **required** to close an engagement. you can't mark a job complete without rating the consultant first.

## the five dimensions

| dimension | what it captures |
|---|---|
| **work product quality** | did the deliverable meet expectations? |
| **communication** | were responses timely and clear? |
| **timeliness** | did they hit the agreed timeline? |
| **professionalism** | how was the overall working relationship? |
| **cost value** | was the price worth what you got? |

each is rated **0.5 to 5.0 stars in half-step increments** (so 4.5, 3.0, 1.5 are all valid).

an optional written **endorsement** (up to 280 characters) lets you add a short note. published quotes appear on the consultant's profile.

## how a consultant's overall rating is computed

```
overall = average of all five dimension averages across all reviews
```

so a consultant with two reviews gets the per-dimension averages calculated first, then those averaged together. one bad review doesn't tank the score, but it doesn't get hidden either.

reviews with **no rating data submitted** (e.g., the engagement was canceled before completion) don't count.

## visibility

| where it shows | who sees it |
|---|---|
| consultant's public profile | anyone signed in |
| marketplace search ranking | feeds the underlying score |
| stage progression bar | the consultant only |

written endorsements display the **owner's display name + the listing title** (truncated). the bid amount isn't shown.

## ratings drive stage progression

your overall rating is one of the two inputs (the other is engagement count) that determines your stage on the seedling → sequoia ladder. see [stage progression](./stage-progression.md) for the full mechanic.

a quick read: you need to maintain a **4.0+ average** across at least a few engagements to advance past seedling.

## editing or removing a review

reviews are final once submitted. there's no edit button on grassroots.

if a review contains something inappropriate (harassment, identifying private info, etc.), email [moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com](mailto:moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com) — we'll review and remove case-by-case.

## faq

- **can i see who left a review?** the owner's display name and the listing title are shown. their full profile isn't linked from the endorsement.
- **what counts as a "no rating"?** an engagement that ended without an owner-submitted review — for example, canceled before kickoff. these don't affect averages.
- **does grassroots show the lowest-rated consultants in search?** they appear, but they rank lower. we don't hide consultants below a threshold; we want owners to see real signal.
- **can the consultant respond to a review?** not currently. we may add a reply feature later if there's demand.
