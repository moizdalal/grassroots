# Accounts & roles

> Grassroots has two account types: **consultants** and **business owners**. You pick one at registration and the rest of the platform reshapes around the choice.

## At a glance

| | Consultant | Business owner |
|---|---|---|
| Who it's for | Someone offering services | Someone hiring for a project |
| Home page | Consultant dashboard | Owner dashboard |
| Main action | Submit bids on listings | Post listings, review bids |
| Payouts setup | Required (via Stripe) | Not needed |
| Reviews | Gets rated | Gives ratings |
| Stages | Yes — seedling → sequoia | n/a |

## What a consultant can do

- Browse the marketplace and bid on open listings
- Chat with a business owner once a bid is under review
- Pick a kickoff meeting time inside the owner's posted availability
- Get rated on five dimensions when work wraps
- Climb a five-stage progression that affects search ranking

## What a business owner can do

- Post listings describing a project
- Review incoming bids, accept / decline / counter
- Pay at acceptance (funds route to the consultant)
- Chat through the engagement
- Rate the consultant when work wraps — this is required to close the engagement

## Changing your role

A single account can only hold one role. If you need both, create a separate account with a different email.

## One account, one identity

You sign in once and the platform routes you to the right surfaces. The side nav, dashboard, settings tabs, and notifications all branch based on role.
