# Listings

> A listing is a business owner's description of work they want done. It sits in the marketplace, collects bids, and eventually becomes one (or zero) engagements.

## Creating a listing

From your dashboard, click **Post a listing**. You'll fill in:

- **Title** — short and specific ("brand identity for a coffee shop", not "design help")
- **Category** — pick from the predefined list (marketing, operations, etc.)
- **Business problem** — the core ask
- **Standards of work** — what success looks like (deliverables, formats, expectations)
- **Additional details** — context, links, attachments
- **Qualifications** — what you want in a consultant (years of experience, specific skills)
- **Budget range** — a min and max in USD
- **Turnaround days** — how long until you need it done

Once posted, your listing is **open** and visible to all consultants.

## Listing states

| State | Meaning | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| `open` | Accepting bids | Everyone signed in |
| `paused` | Temporarily hidden | Only the owner |
| `closed` | Done — either work completed or withdrawn | Only the owner + accepted consultant |

## Pausing

Owners can pause a listing at any time. Paused listings:
- Disappear from the marketplace
- Don't accept new bids
- Keep existing bids in place — you can resume any time

Useful if you're swamped reviewing offers and don't want more piling up.

## Closing

A listing is closed when:
- You accept a bid (the listing auto-closes; remaining bids resolve to `declined`)
- You manually close it before accepting any bid — you'll be asked why (`work completed`, `found a solution elsewhere`, `didn't find a good offer`, `other`)

Once closed, the listing is read-only.

## Attachments

You can attach files to a listing (briefs, brand guides, mocks). Consultants can see attachments before bidding.

Attachments are private to signed-in users — they're not search-indexed or publicly linkable.

## Editing

Basic fields (description, qualifications, attachments) can be edited any time before acceptance. The title, category, and budget range lock once the first bid arrives, to avoid bait-and-switch on consultants who've already responded.

## FAQ

- **How long do listings stay open?** Indefinitely — you close them manually when you're done.
- **Can I delete a listing?** You can close it. Deletion isn't supported because we keep listings for audit + 1099 history.
- **Do consultants see my bids from other consultants?** No — every consultant sees only their own bid and the listing content.
- **Can I edit a listing after accepting a bid?** No, the listing is locked. Open a new listing for follow-up work.
