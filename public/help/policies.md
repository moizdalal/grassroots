# policies

> ground rules that keep grassroots usable and trustworthy. short for now — we'll expand as edge cases come up.

## content rules

what's not allowed on listings, bids, profiles, or messages:

- **harassment** — slurs, threats, targeted abuse
- **identifying private info** about another user (doxxing)
- **spam** — repeated identical posts, off-topic promotions
- **fraud or misrepresentation** — fake credentials, impersonation
- **illegal work** — anything that would put the consultant or business at legal risk
- **sexual content** — grassroots is not a platform for that work

violations land you a warning, a temporary suspension, or a permanent ban depending on severity. moderation is human + manual today.

## scope abuse

consultants and business owners are both expected to keep work inside what was agreed. specifically:

- **scope creep**: owners can't expand the deliverable after acceptance. add new work as a new listing.
- **out-of-platform payments**: don't route payments outside grassroots. it bypasses stripe's protection and 1099 issuance, and is a violation that ends accounts.
- **side-channel solicitation**: don't poach a consultant off-platform after meeting them through grassroots.

if either side feels the scope is being abused, contact us — we'll mediate or unwind the engagement.

## refunds

formal refund policy is in flight. current posture:

- **before kickoff**: full refund on request, no questions
- **after kickoff, before completion**: case-by-case — typically prorated to the work delivered
- **after the engagement is marked released**: no refund through grassroots. the consultant has been credited.

refund requests go to [help@grassroots.com](mailto:help@grassroots.com) with the bid id.

## disputes

if owner and consultant disagree on whether work was satisfactory:

1. try to resolve directly through the inbox first
2. if no agreement in 7 days, contact help@grassroots.com with both perspectives
3. grassroots will review the conversation, deliverables, and any timestamps, and make a binding call

we don't take sides by default. evidence in the platform (messages, attachments, kickoff notes) is what we lean on.

## reviews

reviews must be honest. retaliation reviews — leaving a low score because the other party left a low score, without engagement-level substance — get removed.

reviews are not editable. if you submitted one with mistakes, contact us.

## privacy

what we store:
- account info (name, email, phone, role)
- listings, bids, messages, reviews
- payment metadata via stripe (we don't see card numbers)
- profile content you upload (photo, resume, portfolio)

what we don't:
- card or bank details (stripe holds these)
- analytics on what you click outside grassroots
- third-party tracking pixels

you can request your data or account deletion any time by emailing help@grassroots.com. payment + 1099 records are retained 7 years per irs requirements.

## reporting

found something wrong? email **help@grassroots.com** with:
- what happened
- a link or id (bid id, listing url, etc.)
- screenshots if relevant

we read every report and respond within 2 business days.
