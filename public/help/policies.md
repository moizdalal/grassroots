# Policies

> Ground rules that keep Grassroots usable and trustworthy. This is a summary of the [terms of service](/terms.html). If anything here differs, the terms win.

## Content rules

What's not allowed on listings, bids, profiles, or messages:

- **Harassment** — slurs, threats, targeted abuse
- **Identifying private info** about another user (doxxing)
- **Spam** — repeated identical posts, off-topic promotions
- **Fraud or misrepresentation** — fake credentials, impersonation
- **Illegal work** — anything that would put the consultant or business at legal risk
- **Sexual content** — Grassroots is not a platform for that work

Violations land you a warning, a temporary suspension, or a permanent ban depending on severity. Moderation is human + manual today.

## Scope abuse

Consultants and business owners are both expected to keep work inside what was agreed. Specifically:

- **Scope creep**: owners can't expand the deliverable after acceptance. Add new work as a new listing.
- **Out-of-platform payments**: don't route payments outside Grassroots. It skips the payment hold, refunds, and tax reporting, and it ends accounts.
- **Side-channel solicitation**: for 12 months after you meet someone on Grassroots, don't move paid work with them off-platform. See [section 11 of the terms](/terms.html#non-circumvention).

If either side feels the scope is being abused, contact us — we'll mediate or unwind the engagement.

## Refunds

This is the same policy as [section 7 of the terms](/terms.html#refunds). If anything here differs, the terms win.

1. **Within 24 hours (one-time jobs):** cancel from the engagement page for a full refund. This only works while the payment is still held.
2. **After 24 hours, before release:** report a problem from the engagement page during your 14-day review, or email [moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com](mailto:moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com) with the bid ID. This is also the only route for proposal fees, service fees, and retainer periods. The payment locks while we review. We then decide one of three things:
   - **The work meets scope:** it's released to the consultant.
   - **It falls short but can be fixed:** the consultant has 5 days to redo it once, or give the money back. A give-back refunds you minus card-processing fees. No answer in 5 days counts as a give-back.
   - **Scope was breached:** you get a full refund. The consultant gets nothing.
3. **After release:** no refund through Grassroots. The payment is final. Any return is between you and the consultant.
4. **Retainers:** a period that's already been charged isn't refunded when either side ends the retainer. There's no proration.
5. **14-day review window:** once the consultant marks the work delivered, you have 14 days to release it, request changes, or report a problem. After that it releases to the consultant automatically. You get a heads-up on day 7. For retainers, each period's 14 days start when the period ends.

Refunds land on your original card, usually within 5–10 business days.

## Disputes

This matches [section 21 of the terms](/terms.html#user-disputes).

1. Try to sort it out in the inbox first.
2. No agreement after 7 days? Email [moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com](mailto:moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com) with the bid ID and both sides.
3. We review the messages, deliverables, and timestamps, and mediate. Mediation isn't binding.
4. If we're still holding the payment, we resolve it the same way as a reported problem (see refunds above). That decision covers the held money only.
5. Contact us before filing a chargeback. While a card dispute is open, we don't release that payment.

We don't take sides by default. Evidence in the platform is what we lean on.

## Reviews

Reviews must be honest. We don't remove reviews for being negative. We only remove ones that break the criteria in [section 15 of the terms](/terms.html#reviews), like harassment, private info, spam, or pure retaliation with nothing about the work.

Reviews are not editable. If you submitted one with mistakes, contact us.

## Privacy

What we store:
- Account info (name, email, phone, role)
- Listings, bids, messages, reviews
- Payment metadata via Stripe (we don't see card numbers)
- Profile content you upload (photo, resume, portfolio)

What we don't:
- Card or bank details (Stripe holds these)
- Analytics on what you click outside Grassroots
- Third-party tracking pixels

You can request your data or account deletion from settings, or by emailing moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com. Payment and tax records are kept 7 years. The full detail is in the [privacy policy](/privacy.html).

## Reporting

Found something wrong? Email **moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com** with:
- What happened
- A link or ID (bid ID, listing URL, etc.)
- Screenshots if relevant

We read every report and respond within 2 business days.
