1. Who we are

Grassroots is a marketplace operated by Grassroots Marketplace LLC (referred to in this policy as "Grassroots," "we," "us," or "our"). Our business address is 7901 4th St N, Ste 300, St. Petersburg, FL 33702, USA. You can contact us at moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com.

We provide a two-sided marketplace where small businesses post project listings and student consultants submit proposals. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website, account system, messaging, and payment processing. Your use of Grassroots is also governed by our Terms of Service.

This policy applies to information collected through grassroots-marketplace.com and any associated subdomains we operate.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you give us

When you create an account, post a listing, submit a bid, or otherwise interact with our service, we collect:

  • Account information: first and last name, email address, phone number, password (stored as a salted hash), and city.
  • Role-specific details (business owners): business name, business address, website URL, social media links, and a business description.
  • Instagram profile photo (optional): if you add an Instagram URL and ask us to use your Instagram photo as your avatar, we request your public Instagram profile picture from Instagram and store a copy as your avatar.
  • Role-specific details (student consultants): school, major, minor, education background, resume file, portfolio items, and LinkedIn URL.
  • Marketplace content: listings you post, bids you submit, messages you exchange with other users, reviews you write, scheduling availability, and any attachments you upload.
  • Support and feedback: messages you send to our support email, in-product feedback you submit through the suggestion form, and the page URL and browser identifier captured automatically when you submit feedback.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, and approximate location derived from your IP, used for security and platform analytics.
  • Performance metrics: anonymous page-load timing (LCP, INP, CLS, TTFB, FCP) and device class, used to monitor site performance. This data is not tied to your identity.
  • Listing view tracking: when you view a marketplace listing while signed in, we record an entry tying your user ID to that listing so owners can see view counts on their listings. Owners cannot identify individual viewers from this data.

2.3 Payment information

When you pay for or receive payment for work on the marketplace, payment processing is handled by Stripe, Inc. We do not receive or store full payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or tax identification numbers. We store identifiers issued by Stripe (such as a customer ID, connected-account ID, payment intent ID, charge ID, and transfer ID) that allow us to associate marketplace activity with the corresponding Stripe records.

Student consultants who set up payouts complete a Stripe Connect onboarding flow in which Stripe collects identity-verification information directly. We do not see or receive that information; please review Stripe's privacy notice for details.

3. How we use it

We use the information we collect to:

  • operate the marketplace — create accounts, display listings, route messages, schedule engagements, and facilitate payments;
  • match business owners with consultants whose skills, school, or location are relevant to a listing;
  • process payments and payouts through Stripe, including holding payments until they are released, releases, refunds, and retainer billing;
  • communicate with you about account activity (sign-in confirmations, password resets, listing updates, bid status, and engagement milestones);
  • provide customer support and respond to questions you send us;
  • maintain the security and integrity of the platform, including fraud prevention and abuse investigation;
  • understand how the product is used in aggregate so we can improve it;
  • comply with legal obligations such as tax reporting, audit, and responding to lawful requests from government authorities.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information to train machine-learning models for third parties.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the service providers we use to operate the platform ("sub-processors"), and only as needed for them to provide their service to us. Each sub-processor is contractually required to handle data securely and to use it only on our instructions.

Sub-processor Purpose Privacy notice
Supabase, Inc. Database, authentication, file storage, and backend infrastructure. supabase.com/privacy
Stripe, Inc. Payment processing, Stripe Connect for consultant payouts, fraud detection. stripe.com/privacy
Vercel, Inc. Web hosting, content delivery, and platform-level security. vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
Resend, Inc. Sending notification emails (for example bid, payment, and engagement updates). Receives your email address, name, and the content of the notification. resend.com/legal/privacy-policy

We also send limited information to these third-party services, which use it under their own policies rather than as our sub-processors:

  • U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder — when a business owner saves a listing, we send the business address to the Census Bureau's public geocoding service to get map coordinates for local search. Only the address is sent. See census.gov privacy policy.
  • Instagram (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — only if you use the Instagram avatar feature, we send your Instagram username to Instagram to fetch your public profile picture. See Instagram's privacy policy.

We may also share information when required to do so by law (for example, in response to a valid subpoena), when needed to investigate fraud or abuse, or in connection with a corporate transaction (such as a merger or acquisition) — in which case we will notify affected users where required.

Other users of the platform see only the information we describe in Section 6. We do not show your email address, phone number, password, or payment details to other users.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use a small set of browser storage mechanisms to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising cookies.

5.2 Third-party cookies (Stripe)

When you reach a payment checkout, our payment provider Stripe loads its JavaScript library into the page. Stripe sets the cookies __stripe_mid and __stripe_sid to detect and prevent fraud. These are essential for processing payments and we cannot disable them. They are governed by Stripe's cookie policy.

Stripe.js is only loaded on pages where a checkout is initiated; it is not loaded on browsing-only pages such as the home page, marketplace, or this policy.

5.3 How to clear browser storage

You can clear all local storage and cookies set by our site through your browser's settings ("Clear browsing data" or "Site settings"). Doing so will sign you out and reset your preferences.

6. Public information

Some information on Grassroots is intentionally visible to anyone who visits the site, including visitors who are not signed in. By posting content to the marketplace, you agree to that content being publicly visible:

  • Open marketplace listings — the listing title, category, problem summary, details, attachments, qualifications, and turnaround for any listing in "open" status are viewable by unauthenticated visitors.
  • Business details — for a business with an open listing, the owner's name and photo and the business's name, description, website, social links, and business address are viewable by anyone, including visitors who are not signed in.
  • Consultant names and photos — a consultant's name, photo, and LinkedIn URL are visible to the business owners whose listings they have bid on or messaged.
  • Consultant profile details — a consultant's school and education details, skills, and any portfolio items or resume the consultant marks as visible are shown only to business owners the consultant has sent an offer or a message to. They are not shown to the public or to other consultants.
  • Avatars — profile photos uploaded to the platform are stored in a publicly accessible bucket so that they can be displayed efficiently. Anyone with the URL can view a profile photo.
  • Reviews — reviews you write are displayed on the consultant's profile to the business owners who can view it. If you mark a review as anonymous, your name will not appear next to it.

Private information — including your email address, phone number, Stripe account identifiers, message history (other than with the people you message), scheduling details, and payment records — is not visible to other users or to the public. A business owner sees the offers made on their own listings, and the other party to an engagement sees the details of that engagement.

7. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and for the period afterward described below:

  • Account data — retained while your account is active. If you request deletion, we will retain a minimal record (your user ID and the fact that you deleted your account) for up to 30 days to allow for recovery in case of mistake or dispute, after which we will purge the account.
  • Transaction records — Stripe records and platform records of completed payments are retained for at least seven years to comply with United States tax and financial-reporting requirements.
  • Messages and reviews — retained for as long as your account is active. On account deletion, we anonymize messages and reviews you wrote (your name is removed and replaced with "deleted user") so that the conversation history remains coherent for the other party, but your identity is no longer attached.
  • Uploaded files — resumes, portfolio items, listing attachments, and proposals are deleted from storage when you delete your account or remove the item, with the exception of files attached to completed transactions that are subject to the financial-records retention period above.
  • Performance metrics — anonymous performance data is retained for up to 13 months for trend analysis.

We may retain information longer if required by law (for example, in response to a legal hold) or if it is necessary to resolve a dispute or enforce our agreements.

8. Your choices and rights

You have the following choices about your information:

  • Access and correction. You can view and edit most of your personal information directly in your settings page (name, phone, city, business or education details, resume, portfolio items, and so on).
  • Account deletion. You can request deletion of your account from the Security tab in your settings. We process deletion requests within 14 days. Some information may be retained as described in Section 7.
  • Data export. You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you by submitting an export request from your settings page or by emailing moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com. We will provide your data in a portable format within 30 days.
  • Communications. Transactional emails (about your account, your listings, bids, payments, and security) cannot be opted out of while you have an active account, because they are essential to using the service. We do not currently send marketing emails. If we begin to, every marketing message will include an unsubscribe link.
  • Cookies. See Section 5 for how to clear browser storage.

8.1 Rights under state privacy laws

Residents of certain U.S. states (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and Virginia) have additional rights under state privacy laws, including the right to access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of their information, and the right to opt out of certain forms of data processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

We do not "sell" personal information or "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

9. Children

Grassroots is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us at moiz@grassroots-marketplace.com and we will delete the account.

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account on Grassroots, as set out in our Terms of Service.

10. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information:

  • data in transit is encrypted with TLS;
  • data at rest is encrypted by our database provider (Supabase) and by Stripe;
  • access to user data is enforced at the database level through row-level security and column-level permissions, which limit each user to their own records, the records of engagements and conversations they are part of, and the profile information described in Section 6; payment status and Stripe identifiers can only be changed by our servers;
  • passwords are stored as salted hashes by our authentication provider and are never visible to us;
  • administrative access to systems is limited to a small number of authorized personnel.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify you and any required authorities within the timeframes required by applicable law.

11. International users

Grassroots is intended for users in the United States. Our servers and data infrastructure are located in the United States, and the laws of the United States govern the processing of personal information through our service.

We do not currently offer the service to users in the European Union, the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, or Switzerland. Visitors from those regions may be redirected to a notice page. If you believe you are receiving this notice in error, please contact us.
